01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

A bakery storefront shaped as a complete digital experience.

The live Gul Bakes site currently presents category-led shopping, best-selling cakes, custom cake ordering, brand content, customer-care pages and blog content. The project combines storefront setup with the surrounding marketing and management work needed to present the brand professionally.

What the store includes

Product discovery through categories and collections, custom cakes, featured products, brand-story content, customer reviews, blog content, contact and customer-care pages.
